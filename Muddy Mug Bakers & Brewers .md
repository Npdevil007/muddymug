## From the classroom.

What students work with: our espresso calibration board and the coffee cherry, layer by layer.

### Espresso calibration

Whiteboard titled Espresso Calibration listing dose, volume, ratio, yield and extraction time for Italian and specialty standards

#### Italian standard

Dose **7–8 g**

Volume **30 ml**

Extraction **18–23 sec**

#### Specialty standard

Dose **9 g**

Ratio **1:2**

Yield **18 g**

Extraction **18–23 sec**

Double shot: 25–30 sec.
